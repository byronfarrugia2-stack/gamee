THE LAST TRANSIT

Halloway Observatory, 1893. 10:40 p.m.

Professor Halloway promised you a front-row seat to the return of Comet Vesper, a once-in-a-lifetime visitor. Then the great door swung shut behind you. Its clockwork lock is wound to release only for the transit, and the transit ends at dawn. After sunrise the door will not open again for seventy-six years.

The Professor is nowhere to be found. His notes, instruments and half-finished sums are scattered around the dome. Read them, wake the instruments, and open the door before the sky turns gold.

HOW TO PLAY
• WASD to walk, mouse to look, click glowing things to use them.
• Opening a note or instrument frees your mouse. Close it, then click the room once to look around again.
• Brass-framed notes are clues. Read them all.
• Wrong answers move the dawn closer.
• Press H for an optional hint for the current task.

DAWN BREAKS

Gold light spills through the shutters and the lock winds itself shut with a heavy clunk. The comet has come and gone. Somewhere in the walls, seventy-six years begin to tick.

THE DOOR OPENS

The vault door groans upward and cold night air rushes in. Behind you, Comet Vesper blazes across the shutters, exactly on time. You step out into the dark with the stars still overhead. You are free, and you never did find the Professor.
